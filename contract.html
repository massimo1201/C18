Contract

We work alongside architecture firms, general contractors, and companies on turnkey projects — from space planning to supply and installation, coordinating executive, operative, meeting rooms, and acoustics into a single vision.

How We Work

Space Planning

We work with your design team from the earliest layout stage, aligning furniture systems with the architecture of the space.

Supply

Every piece is manufactured in-house at our Friuli Venezia Giulia facilities, keeping quality and lead times consistent across the whole project.

Installation

Our logistics and installation teams coordinate delivery and assembly on site, in Italy and internationally.
