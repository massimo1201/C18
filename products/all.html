Company

Since 1954, Codutti has designed and manufactured office furniture entirely in-house, uniting seventy years of Italian craftsmanship with the precision of modern technology to build tailor-made workspaces for clients around the world. VAT IT14045811008.

Headquarters — Pasian di Prato (UD), Italy Production Site — Martignacco (UD), Italy
Our Products

Desks, Meetings, Seatings, Coffee Tables, Storage Units, Receptions and Acoustic Solutions — a complete range for every space. Explore Our Products.

Contract

Turnkey furnishing for offices and large-scale projects of every kind, coordinated end to end by our dedicated contract team. Discover Contract.

Bespoke

Made-to-measure pieces designed around your space, materials and brand identity, produced entirely in our Friuli Venezia Giulia workshops. Discover Bespoke.

Work with Us

Terms of Sale

Our general terms of sale cover quotes, orders, custom production lead times, and delivery. Contract and bespoke supplies are governed by a dedicated agreement with specific timelines and specifications.

Warranty & Support

Every Codutti collection is covered by a warranty on materials and workmanship. Our after-sales service covers installation, maintenance, and spare parts for the product's entire lifecycle.

Shipping & Installation

Delivery, assembly, and installation are coordinated by the Codutti logistics team across Italy and internationally, with particular attention to large-scale contract projects.

Certifications

Made in Italy

Production takes place entirely in Friuli Venezia Giulia. Wood and metal components are made with CNC machines, while leather finishes are stitched strictly by hand.
