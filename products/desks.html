Desks

Discover the iSixty Collection

Our desks are built to serve the full life of a workspace, not just the boardroom. At one end of the range, the Executive line brings solid wood veneers, hand-finished leather details and sculptural forms to offices where every surface is meant to represent the company. At the other, Operative and Adjustable desks trade ornament for endurance — resilient laminates, integrated cable management and modular frames shaped for the everyday pace of open-plan teams. Between these two ends lies over a decade of manufacturing experience delivered from UTC's Riyadh workshops, tuning each line to the space and the budget it's meant to serve.
