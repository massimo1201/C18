Desks

Executive and operative desks engineered for the way modern teams work — solid wood veneers, hand-finished leather details, and modular systems that scale from a single office to an entire floor. Every desk in this range is built in-house, combining CNC precision with traditional craftsmanship.

Since 1954, the desk has been the piece every Codutti project starts from — the first thing the workshop in Fagagna learned to build well. The joinery techniques behind today's executive and operative lines are the same ones passed down through three generations of the Codutti family, now combined with CNC precision to keep every desk consistent across an entire floor.
