Acoustic Solutions

Acoustic solutions are the newest chapter in UTC's range, born from client requests for privacy and sound control as offices opened up into shared floor plans. The pods and panels are built using the same in-house upholstery and panel-forming skills the company has relied on since day one, applied to a new generation of enclosed and freestanding systems that manage sound without breaking design continuity with the rest of the space.
