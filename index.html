We're Codutti:
more than 70 years of workspaces.

The story of Codutti began in 1954, when three brothers joined forces in a modest village workshop. Since then, Codutti has built a legacy of quality and prestige, becoming a benchmark brand in the luxury furniture market. Attention to detail and product reliability led the company to provide the furnishings for two icons of the Italian fleet in the 1960s: the Raffaello and Michelangelo cruise ships. Over the years, Codutti has refined its passion for producing high-end furniture, specializing particularly in the office sector — introducing meticulous craftsmanship in leather and hides alongside its traditional wooden offerings.

04 — Method

The Codutti Method

Codutti's production method guarantees the high quality and prestige of its office furniture by blending advanced technology with artisanal craftsmanship. Wooden and metal components are shaped with CNC machines for precision, while leather details are hand-finished following traditional techniques.
