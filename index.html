70 years plus of workspaces manufacturing

The story of Codutti began in the 1950s, when three brothers joined forces in a modest village workshop. Since then, Codutti has built a legacy of quality and prestige, becoming a benchmark brand in the luxury furniture market. Attention to detail and product reliability led the company to provide the furnishings for two icons of the Italian fleet in the 1960s: the Raffaello and Michelangelo cruise ships. Over the years, Codutti has refined its passion for producing high-end furniture, specializing particularly in the office sector — introducing meticulous craftsmanship in leather and hides alongside its traditional wooden offerings.

Contemporary Vision

To be the international benchmark for Made-in-Italy luxury executive furniture — a reference point where seventy years of craftsmanship meet the precision of new technology. We look at the evolving nature of work and translate it into pieces that feel both contemporary and enduring, designed to represent the ambitions of the people who use them every day.

Crafted Mission

To transform every office into a high-end space, uniting functionality and impeccable aesthetics in furniture built to last. Every desk, table, and system we craft is conceived as part of a larger environment — one where comfort, performance, and beauty are never treated as separate goals, but as a single, coherent idea of quality.

Contract Values

Uncompromising quality, attention to detail, respect for materials and people, and a deep sense of Italian roots guide every decision we make — from the selection of raw materials to the relationships we build with clients and partners. These values have shaped Codutti since 1954 and continue to define how we work today.

The Codutti Method

Codutti's production method guarantees the high quality and prestige of its office furniture by blending advanced technology with artisanal craftsmanship. Wooden and metal components are shaped with CNC machines for precision, while leather details are hand-finished following traditional techniques.

Essential

The entry point to the Codutti world: clean lines and durable materials built for everyday operative spaces, where comfort and function come together without compromise. Essential brings together seating, tables, storage and acoustic systems designed to furnish fast-moving offices without ever feeling generic — a dependable, versatile base for teams of every size, ready to be configured room by room as needs change. It is the collection Codutti recommends when a project calls for consistent quality across many workstations at once, without sacrificing the comfort and finish that carry through every other line in the catalogue.

Operative

Furniture designed for shared, dynamic workspaces — desks, benches and adjustable systems shaped around teamwork and the way modern offices actually move. Operative collections favour open, reconfigurable layouts and height-adjustable solutions that let teams shift between focused work and collaboration throughout the day, without compromising on the durability and finish Codutti is known for. Every piece is engineered to be reconfigured as teams grow and layouts change, making it the natural choice for fast-scaling companies and coworking environments that need to stay flexible without looking temporary.

Executive

Codutti's flagship collection for management offices and boardrooms — director desks and executive furniture crafted for a refined, high-end presence. Every piece is built in-house from wood, metal and leather, combining CNC precision with hand-finished details to give directors and senior teams a workspace that reflects the standing of the organisation they represent. This is where Codutti's seventy years of craftsmanship are most visible, in details like hand-stitched leather panels, solid wood veneers and hardware finished to match each client's interior specification.
