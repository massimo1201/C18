Bespoke

Codutti offers the possibility to customise each element, providing added value that goes well beyond mere functionality. This custom-made approach allows for a work environment that not only meets practical needs, but is also a true symbol of elegance and professionalism.

Our Customisation Process

Every bespoke project starts with a conversation about how a space will actually be used, followed by material and finish selection, technical drawings, and in-house production — the same CNC precision and hand-finished leather work behind every standard Codutti collection, applied to a one-of-a-kind piece.

What Can Be Customised

Materials & Finishes

Solid wood veneers, metal finishes, and a wide range of leathers and fabrics.

Dimensions

Desks, tables and storage systems sized to the exact footprint of your space.

Details

Hand-stitched leather panels, hardware and trims finished to match each client's interior specification.
