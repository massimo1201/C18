70 years plus of workspaces manufacturing

The story of Codutti began in the 1950s, when three brothers joined forces in a modest village workshop. Since then, Codutti has built a legacy of quality and prestige, becoming a benchmark brand in the luxury office furniture market — specialising in Essential, Operative and Executive collections designed and manufactured entirely in-house.

Who We Are

Attention to detail and product reliability led the company to provide the furnishings for two icons of the Italian fleet in the 1960s: the Raffaello and Michelangelo cruise ships. Over the years, Codutti has refined its passion for producing high-end furniture, specialising particularly in the office sector — introducing meticulous craftsmanship in leather and hides alongside its traditional wooden offerings.

Today Codutti designs, engineers and manufactures every collection at its own production facilities in Friuli Venezia Giulia, combining CNC precision on wood and metal components with hand-finished leather detailing — a method that lets us customise nearly every element of a project without compromising on consistency or lead time.

History & Brand Evolution

1954

Three brothers found the original workshop in Friuli Venezia Giulia, laying the foundations of what would become Codutti.

1960s

Codutti furnishes the Raffaello and Michelangelo — two icons of the Italian cruise fleet — establishing the brand as a benchmark for quality and prestige.

1980s–90s

The company deepens its specialisation in the office sector, expanding in-house production of executive and operative furniture.

2000s

Introduction of contract and bespoke project capabilities, supporting architecture firms and general contractors on large-scale turnkey fit-outs worldwide.

2020s

Launch of the Essential, Operative and Executive collection architecture, and continued investment in acoustic solutions and adjustable systems for the modern office.

2026

Codutti continues to exhibit internationally — from Fuori Salone in Milan to Clerkenwell Design Week in London — while publishing the Codutti Journal on design, craftsmanship and workspace culture.
