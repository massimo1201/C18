A decade of design and build in Saudi Arabia

UTC — United Technology for Construction — was founded in 2014 as a Naif Al-Rajhi Investment company. Since then, UTC has built a reputation for turnkey design and build delivery, specialising in Design & Engineer, Construction & Execution, Stainless Steel & Wood Works, Furniture & Industrial Kitchens, and Physical Security Technologies, coordinated entirely in-house.

Who We Are

UTC delivers innovative turnkey solutions for commercial spaces, residential properties and the F&B sector across Saudi Arabia. Over the years, UTC has grown its capabilities beyond construction into stainless steel and wood works, furniture and industrial kitchens, and physical security technologies — a broader scope than a typical contractor, built to remove handoffs between design and delivery.

Today UTC designs, engineers, constructs and fits out projects from its own team in Riyadh, combining engineering precision with hands-on execution — an approach that lets us coordinate nearly every element of a project without compromising on consistency or lead time. Furniture and industrial kitchen supply is delivered through UTC's sister company, First Furniture.
