Ginza

Named for the precision of its namesake district, Ginza is Codutti's most minimal executive desk — a thin wood or lacquered top on a fine metal frame, built for leaders who prefer restraint over ornament. It pairs naturally with Codutti's Executive seating and storage lines for a fully coordinated office.
